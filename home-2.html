Streamlined Roadside Workflow

The Field Technician Dispatch Sequence

From initial call to driving away with a programmed key, here is how our professional dispatch operates.

1

GPS Live Tracking

Your service request is assigned to the nearest patrol van. You receive an SMS tracking link with the technician's name and accurate ETA.

2

Key Decode & VIN Check

The technician verifies vehicle ownership and reads the ignition mechanical key bitting using optical or Lishi precision decoders.

3

CNC Milling & Pairing

The van's CNC laser cutter creates a factory-fresh blade while the OBD diagnostic tablet pairs the new RFID transponder chip.

4

Quality Assurance

We test proximity door lock/unlock, trunk pop, push-button ignition, and emergency physical door locks before finalizing.
