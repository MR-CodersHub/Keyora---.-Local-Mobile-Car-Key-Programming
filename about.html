Our Story & Heritage

Born To Solve The Frustrating Dealership Lockout Trap

In 2011, Keyora was founded with a singular mission: free drivers from the extortionate pricing, long waiting lines, and towing hassles of traditional car dealerships when dealing with lost or broken car keys.

What started as a single service van has evolved into the region's largest fleet of mobile key cutting and electronic immobilizer programming workshops. Today, our certified technicians dispatch 24/7 across the metropolitan area, delivering dealership-quality keys at curbside speed.
